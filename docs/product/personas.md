# Personas

## Primary — junior credit analyst

- Knowledge: understands financial statements and common ratios but needs policy guidance and reviewable formulas.
- Jobs: spread financials, normalize earnings and cash flow, size a facility, test downside resilience, propose terms, and draft a traceable recommendation.
- Common errors: treating EBITDA as cash flow, using a naive debt-service-times-years capacity formula, overlooking refinancing concentration, accepting unsupported add-backs, or reading NM as zero.
- Required explanations: plain-language label first; formula, threshold, period, source, confidence factors, and override history on demand.
- Information tolerance: summary-first on Home and Case Summary; full analyst artifacts on Financials/Risk/Stress/Terms.
- Success: can identify the decision, binding constraint, repayment source, major risks, and supporting calculations without leaving the case.

## Secondary — recruiter or interviewer

- Knowledge: variable; may spend less than five minutes.
- Jobs: understand the product in ten seconds, open a complete sample in one click, see credible logic, and inspect engineering depth.
- Common errors: assuming synthetic data is public-company fact or mistaking an internal grade for an agency rating.
- Required explanations: pinned synthetic label, educational disclaimer, visible binding constraint, methodology link.
- Information tolerance: low initially, high if the project earns attention.
- Success: can retell the product story and demonstrate one case in under five minutes.

## Secondary — finance student

- Knowledge: basic accounting and corporate finance; unfamiliar with bank policy language.
- Jobs: learn how cash flow, debt structure, stress, covenants, and capacity connect to a lending recommendation.
- Common errors: equating collateral with repayment, assuming scenarios are probabilities, or treating score bands as official ratings.
- Required explanations: glossary, `Why?`, formula drawer, deterministic-scenario label, plain-language metric interpretations.
- Information tolerance: moderate with progressive disclosure.
- Success: explains the recommendation and finds deeper evidence without being forced through a dense dashboard.

## Explicit exclusions

- Borrower self-service: a different product centered on qualification estimates.
- Portfolio risk manager: the portfolio page is a supporting demonstration, not the primary workflow.
- Regulator or production bank user: this educational platform is not validated for real decisions or regulatory use.
